This policy explains what information Canvas Digital collects, why we collect it, and how we protect it. It covers both this website and the Canvas Digital SEO Suite, the private platform our team and clients use to run search engine optimisation audits and reporting.

1. Information we collect

1.1 This website

When you browse canvasdigital.net our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamps. These are used to keep the site running and secure. If you contact us through the site, we keep the details you send so we can reply.

1.2 The SEO Suite platform

Access to the platform is by invitation and requires an account. For each account we store:

  • your name and email address;
  • a hashed password (we never store the password itself);
  • the workspace and role you belong to, which determines what you can see;
  • a server-side session record while you are signed in.

We also store the working data of the service itself: the websites being analysed, audit and crawl results, keyword research, generated reports, and tasks assigned to team members.

2. Google user data

The platform connects to Google services so that our analysis reflects real measured data rather than estimates. A connection is made only when an administrator explicitly authorises it through Google’s own consent screen, and it can be withdrawn at any time.

2.1 What we access, and why

Google servicePermission requestedWhat we do with it
Search Console webmasters.readonly Read search performance (queries, impressions, clicks, positions), indexing status and sitemaps for the sites you own, to produce audits and reports.
Google Analytics 4 analytics.readonly Read reporting data such as sessions, engagement and conversions, to relate SEO changes to outcomes.
Google Ads adwords Read keyword planning data — monthly search volume, competition and top-of-page bid estimates — for keyword research and content planning.
Google account userinfo.email, openid Identify which Google account is connected, so the correct account is shown and can be disconnected.

All of these are read-only. The platform never creates, edits, pauses or deletes anything in your Google accounts. In particular, although the Google Ads permission is a broad one — Google offers no narrower option for its Ads API — we use it solely to request keyword ideas and their search volumes. We do not create or manage campaigns, ad groups, keywords, budgets or bids, and we do not spend money through it.

2.2 Google Ads accounts

By default, keyword search volumes are retrieved through Canvas Digital’s own Google Ads account as part of the research we perform for you. Clients are not required to have a Google Ads account. Where a client chooses to connect their own, it is used only for that client’s own keyword research.

2.3 Storage and sharing

  • Data retrieved from Google is stored in our own private database and used only to produce the audits, research and reports for the account it came from.
  • We do not sell Google user data, and we do not transfer it to third parties except as strictly necessary to run the service (see section 5).
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.
  • Access tokens are held on our server, never exposed to your browser, and never committed to any code repository.

Limited Use disclosure

Canvas Digital’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2.4 Disconnecting

You can revoke our access at any time, in either of two places:

  • inside the platform, on the Connected accounts page, using Disconnect Google account; or
  • in your Google account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Disconnecting stops all further access immediately and deletes the stored tokens. Reports already produced remain in your workspace unless you ask us to delete them.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the SEO services you have engaged us for, and to produce the resulting audits and reports.
  • To operate, secure and support the platform, including authenticating users and preventing abuse.
  • To send service messages you have configured, such as alerts about a monitored site and notifications when work is assigned to you.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Legal basis

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we rely on contract (to deliver the services a client has engaged us for), legitimate interests (to run and secure the platform), consent (for any Google connection, which you grant and can withdraw), and legal obligation where the law requires us to retain records.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the service. They process data on our instructions only:

  • our application hosting provider, which stores the platform’s database and files;
  • our website hosting provider, which serves canvasdigital.net;
  • an email delivery provider, used to send alerts and notifications.

Where a provider is outside your country, transfers are made under appropriate safeguards. We do not otherwise disclose your information, except where required by law.

6. Retention

Account records are kept for as long as the account is active. Analysis data, reports and historical performance are kept for the duration of our engagement and for a reasonable period afterwards so that year-on-year comparisons remain possible. Server logs are kept for a short period for security purposes. You may ask us to delete your workspace at any time, and we will do so unless we are legally required to keep specific records.

7. Security

  • The platform is served exclusively over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.
  • Sessions are held server-side and expire.
  • Each workspace can only read its own data.
  • Credentials and API tokens are held in server-side configuration, never in client-side code.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take these measures seriously and review them as the platform changes.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email ahmed.ashraf@canvasdigital.org. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The platform is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting how we handle Google user data will also be communicated to affected account holders directly.
